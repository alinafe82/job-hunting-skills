# Evidence Resume Tailoring

## Workflow

1. Parse the target role into must-have skills, likely screening keywords, responsibilities, seniority signals, and domain context.
2. Inventory the candidate material into verified achievements, tools, domains, leadership scope, and metrics.
3. Map role requirements to evidence. Mark strong matches, partial matches, and missing proof.
4. Rewrite only where evidence supports the claim. Preserve accurate scope and tense.
5. Return targeted edits with a reason for each meaningful change.

## Bullet Standards

- Start with an action verb and state the business or technical outcome.
- Include metrics when supplied; do not manufacture numbers.
- Keep bullets specific enough to distinguish the candidate from a generic applicant.
- Use the role's language when accurate, but avoid keyword stuffing.
- Keep the resume readable for both recruiters and hiring managers.

## Output

Return:

- Role-targeting summary.
- Evidence map.
- Revised bullets or sections.
- Claims to remove or soften.
- Missing facts to ask the user for if they would materially improve the resume.
