# Interview Evidence Pack

## Workflow

1. Identify interview type, role expectations, interviewer context, timing, and evaluation signals.
2. Select evidence-backed stories that map to the role's most important competencies.
3. Prepare answer outlines, not memorized scripts, unless the user asks for exact wording.
4. Create a short question bank for the interviewer, tuned to the stage and recipient.
5. Flag gaps that need research or candidate facts before the interview.

## Story Standards

- Use situation, action, result, and reflection.
- Make the candidate's individual contribution clear.
- Tie technical choices to business or user impact.
- Keep answers concise enough for live conversation.
- Do not over-polish away tradeoffs, failures, or lessons learned.

## Output

Return:

- Interview focus map.
- Likely questions grouped by theme.
- Recommended stories and answer outlines.
- Questions to ask the interviewer.
- Final prep checklist for the next 24 hours.
